FAQ

Questions? Answers.

Is my data stored in Germany?

Yes. All data is hosted in ISO-certified data centres in Germany, and we sign a data processing agreement (AVV) with every customer.

Does it work on my crew’s phones?

Yes – in any modern browser on iPhone and Android, and as an installable app. No app store needed.

Can I switch from Excel or a whiteboard?

We import your customers and recurring jobs for free during the trial. Most teams are up and running in an afternoon.

Is the software available in German?

Of course. Fildercode speaks German and English – your office can use one, your crew the other.
